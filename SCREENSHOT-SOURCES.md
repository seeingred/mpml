# Screenshot sources

All twelve assets in `assets/real/` are real screenshots, downloaded from the sources below or supplied by the site owner. The files are preserved byte-for-byte. SVG crops remove the desktop around Winamp 2 and Apple Music. Runtime SVG patches replace current-track text, clocks, progress, and playback icons; original library content and other artwork remain visible. These composites are adaptations, not untouched screenshots.

The timeline years describe the remembered journey and were deliberately kept as requested. Screenshot versions do not always match those dates. The original generated assets remain in `assets/`, unused by the published screenshot version.

## Known visual compromises

- Winamp 2 and 3 are only 275 pixels wide at native size; Winamp 5 is 357 pixels wide. Enlarging them cannot add detail.
- Winamp 3 is a real capture of the unmodified skin shown in a later Winamp community thread, not a verified capture of the original 2002 executable.
- foobar2000 shows v0.9.6 on Vista, later than the 2004 stop.
- Rhythmbox is Spanish; iTunes 7 is a 2006 Mac capture used for the Leopard chapter.
- Spotify is the 2021 redesign used at the existing 2020 stop.
- Album artwork, other library rows, source usernames, and visualizers are still baked into the captures. They do not describe the selected local song.
- Matching SVG patches are approximate and can be noticeable at large sizes. Morphing remains a continuous crossfade with changing window bounds, not an optical-flow transformation.
